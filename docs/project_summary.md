
# Project Summary

## Problem Statement

Telecom companies can lose revenue when customers discontinue
their subscriptions. This project uses machine learning to estimate
customer churn risk and support retention planning.

## Solution

A machine learning workflow was developed to prepare customer data,
train and compare classification models, generate churn probabilities,
and group customers by risk level.

## Business Analysis

The business impact analysis summarizes customer segments, monthly
revenue estimates, expected revenue at risk, and hypothetical retention
campaign scenarios.

## Dashboard

The Streamlit dashboard provides KPI cards, risk distribution charts,
contract-level analysis, customer search, filters, and CSV export.

## Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic.
- Risk probabilities are model estimates, not certainties.
- Retention ROI depends on assumptions about campaign cost and
  customer contribution.
- Real-world use requires representative data and proper model validation.
